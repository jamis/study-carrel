# Study Carrel: Decisions

Last updated: 2026-10-27

A web app for reading text (scripture, poetry, prose) against a **focus**: a question, metaphor, or thesis ("What does it mean to be holy?" while reading Isaiah 40). The reader sees one passage at a time with the focus pinned above it and writes a note on it.

This is the index. Each line is the rule in force; the linked file has the details, rationale and trade-offs. **Read the topic file before changing that area, and when a decision changes, update both the file and its line here.**

## Always true

- **Stack:** Rails + Hotwire + SQLite; Lexxy 1.0.0 on Action Text for notes. New features are prototyped as plain HTML in `prototype/` first; prototypes aren't kept in the repo (those named in these docs are in git history up to d34f04e).
- **Privacy:** nobody, admins included, can read another user's foci, notes, keeps or texts. User texts go through one visibility scope, and another user gets a 404 everywhere.
- **Notes survive:** loading texts updates in place and never deletes rows notes hang off. Notes whose passage goes away become detached notes, never silently lost.
- **Tone:** a quiet study desk, not a productivity tool. Serif, warm paper, dark mode, no gamification or streaks. Same features on phone and desktop.

## Topics

| Topic | In force | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Foci | One current at a time (the one last made current; the others stay in use); stands directly over the passage, unlabeled soft italic with a thought bubble and a rule across the column, scrolling with it; a chevron drops its description and your other focuses (`o`), to note the passage under one; with neither, nothing to click. Edited from its card, not the reader or its notes page. Home (`/` signed in) is the focuses page: an index card per focus, the current one wide with the way back into reading, each ending with its notes and the works they're in (three at most, those with the most notes, then "and 9 other works"). **Archive** on each card puts one away (with Undo) onto `/foci/archived`, read-only (nothing written, labelled or grouped; enforced on the server) until unarchived; archiving the current leaves none current. **Delete** on each card asks first on a page of its own (the focus, and what goes with it: notes, detached notes, scratchpad; with ways to archive or download instead), then deletes it all, no Undo; deleting the current makes the next most recent current. | [foci-and-notes](docs/decisions/foci-and-notes.md) |
| Notes | One per unit per focus (unique index), plus any number on the focus as a whole (no unit), written in the reread or from the reader sheet's focus tab. Always-open autosaving Lexxy editor docked right / bottom sheet on phones; the sheet's two tabs (`f`), the passage's note and the focus: a list of its notes newest first under a "+ New note" slip (`n`), each opening as an editor in place. Each focus has its own notes page (`/foci/:id/notes`), which is the reread; "Notes on this focus" in the rail, the count on each focus's card; no page across focuses. Notes on the whole focus are numbered on it ("focus 2"), a number never reused. A passage's note can be written under another focus from the list under the focus, without making it current (a slip names it; Esc or a new verse goes back); "Also noted under …" beside the current one's. Detached notes follow the run, read-only with Discard; Copy as Markdown · Download `.md` as quiet words in the page head. | [foci-and-notes](docs/decisions/foci-and-notes.md) |
| Users | A few invited people; single-use invitation links, no email; 30-day sessions; sign out everywhere. | [users](docs/decisions/users.md) |
| Landing page | Public `/` for visitors, with its own layout; no contact address anywhere. | [users](docs/decisions/users.md) |
| Reader | Lectio only: one unit at a time, `‹ n of N ›` and a verse grid; left-aligned, ~34em measure, type steps down for long units. Design reference: `prototype/reader/e.html`. | [reader](docs/decisions/reader.md) |
| Clause by clause | Off by default; `;` turns it on until turned off or the reader is left. The current clause highlighted, the rest dimmed; the arrows step by clause. Split in the browser, for reading only: notes stay with the passage. Breaks chosen under the pips. | [reader](docs/decisions/reader.md) |
| Navigation | One icon rail down the left of every signed-in page, labels shown on hover, no ⋯: Your focuses, Notes on this focus; Go up (the trail) in the reader, Library elsewhere; Recent places, Search (naming where it opens), Random (a popover, "Pick randomly from"), Kept; Continue reading off the reader; you at its foot (light/dark, Invitations, Sign out). The way up above each page's title. One ⋯ top right on phones, under Study and your name. | [reader](docs/decisions/reader.md) |
| Library | Collection (nesting) > Work > Section > Unit; optional `author`/`author_short`; bundled corpus built by `script/texts/`. Users' own collections are private and never mix with the bundled tree. | [library](docs/decisions/library.md) |
| Prose by sentence | Prose is read a sentence at a time; split at build time (`sentences.py`), grouped by paragraph, ¶ view with `p`. | [prose-by-sentence](docs/decisions/prose-by-sentence.md) |
| User texts | Private plain-text uploads (prose, poetry/lyrics or scripture), reviewed before publishing, revisable with loose matching (a revision too different to match in a couple of seconds is refused, to be added as a new text); lost notes become detached notes. Gathered into private collections of their own: made empty from Your Texts (optionally "One work in parts", like the Bible), a text put in one when added; deleting one deletes its texts (notes set aside), after a page saying what goes. | [user-texts](docs/decisions/user-texts.md) |
| History | Global per user, `visits` capped at 50; clock dropdown, `h`. | [history-and-keep](docs/decisions/history-and-keep.md) |
| Keep | Per-user bookmark with optional remark (Save, Enter or a tap away saves it and the toast shows it; Esc leaves it); ribbon by the reference, `b`. A keep needn't have a passage: an **idea** is one with only its remark, something to study someday ("+ Jot an idea", `n`, on the Kept page). Kept page one column, one stream newest first under month heads: passages quoted with their remark, ideas in your words alone, set-aside remarks among them; under each the quiet links Start a focus · Remove, shown on pointing at it; Remove has Undo, no confirm. | [history-and-keep](docs/decisions/history-and-keep.md) |
| Shelves | A user's own private groups of works and whole collections (a collection brings everything under it, nothing twice), to roll Random and search in. Filled from "Put on a shelf…" under a work's or collection's title, or the shelf editor over the library tree. | [shelves](docs/decisions/shelves.md) |
| Random | Scoped (innermost collection by default) and balanced across branches; a shelf is a scope too. | [random](docs/decisions/random.md) |
| Reread | The notes page: a focus's notes one at a time (`/foci/:id/notes`, any focus; `/reread` redirects there): all in one column, each an entry headed by its passage, the reference under it, then the note; the one you're on lit (`j`/`k`) as a band of paper across the page ruled in accent, each note's labels as pills under it; the scratchpad put away behind a tab at the right edge (`s`). Notes on the whole focus written there (`n`, the "+ New note" slip; or "Make a note of this" from the scratchpad). Labels are the user's own, across focuses, put on notes with + label (`l`), a picker showing them all as a cloud (A–Z or recent; the footer says what Enter does); the labels in use as a cloud or tallies on a panel behind the Labels tab (`c`). A pill (or the "8 notes ▾" chip) rereads only its label's notes, a band saying so (×, `Esc` back); there the lit note's pill offers "× Remove", crossed out with Put back until you move on, and the band "Rename" (`r`; "Name it" for an unnamed group). A label's name is optional: **Gather** (`g`) holds the lit note on a small card over the column and lists the focus's other notes, each to put in one of its groups (labels, named or not: an unnamed one goes by a letter, "group D") or a new group of the two; up to three notes held at once under tabs on the card, "Hold this" (Enter) holding another without the list moving; named, renamed (no duplicates, no merging yet) from the card too. | [reread](docs/decisions/reread.md) |
| Search | FTS5 over unit text, every word required, reading order, a sentence at a time for prose; titles and notes not searched. Scoped to a work, collection, Your Texts or a shelf; the rail's Search opens on one (the work, in the reader; else the last searched), and "Somewhere else…" by the scope chips picks another by title. Opening a result puts a "← Back to results" band over the focus while you read on (× or leaving the reader puts it away); Search itself opens empty, offering the last search. | [search](docs/decisions/search.md) |

**Exploratory, not decided:** [discovery](docs/decisions/discovery.md) holds brainstorm notes on helping new users find something to study (an exploring focus, curated lists, keeps in place of notes). Nothing in it is a rule. [synthesis](docs/decisions/synthesis.md) holds notes on turning notes into writing: the reread and notes on the whole focus are decided (above); essays drawn from one or more focuses are still exploratory.

The original brief (v1 scope, open questions, early design implications) is in [origins](docs/decisions/origins.md).
