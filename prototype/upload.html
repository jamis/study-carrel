How the text is read

  1. Paragraphs. Blank lines between paragraphs, and line breaks inside one are joined. With no blank lines at all, every line is a paragraph.
  2. Sections. A line starting #  starts a section, named by the rest of the line. No headings: one section.
  3. Sentences. Each paragraph is read a sentence at a time, like Walden.
  4. A backslash beside . ? ! decides a split: Sgt\. Pepper isn’t an ending, with M.\ We is. \\ is a backslash.
  5. Everything else shows exactly as typed. Asterisks stay asterisks.
