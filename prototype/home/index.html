Home layouts

Signed in, / redirects to the focuses page, which shows every focus you’ve had: the current one with the way back into reading, and past ones to look over or make current again. Layouts over the same six foci; B1–B4 are variations on B’s index cards. “Make current” works in each; the tab on the left edge switches layouts.

ALectern

One column. The current focus large at the top with its description, the passage you stopped at (“Continue at Isaiah 40:25 →”) and your last note; earlier foci below as a quiet list, “Make current” showing on hover.

BIndex cards

A grid of cards, one per focus, each with its dates, works and last note. The current card is twice as wide with a ribbon and the way back in; a dashed card at the end starts a new question. One column on a phone.

B1Ruled cards

True 3×5 cards with blue rules and a red heading line, the notes written on the lines. The current card is larger and shows its latest notes; earlier cards show their last one. ↻ turns a card over: the back has the description, the facts and the actions.

B2Card file

One narrow column, like a card box seen from above. The current card is pulled up in full: description, where to continue, latest notes. The rest stand behind it, showing only their titles and dated tabs staggered in thirds. Pick one to pull it up instead.

B3On the desk

The card in hand sits large at the top (your focus, with a ribbon). The rest of the pile lies loosely below, a little askew; hovering straightens a card, and picking one up puts it in your hand with “Make current”. A blank card starts a new question.

B4Stacks

Each focus is a header card on a stack of its notes. The stack’s edges thicken with the note count, and the latest note peeks out from underneath (it slides out a little on hover). “Spread out the notes” would open them all.

CContents

The foci as a notebook’s table of contents on a single page: numbered i., ii., … grouped by year, dotted leaders to each note count, newest first. An entry opens in place to its description, last note and actions; the current one starts open with a ❧ by it.

DDrawer and desk

Two panes, like a mail reader: every focus in a list on the left, the chosen one in full on the right (counts of notes, keeps and works, where you stopped, the last note, the works read in). On a phone the list comes first and a focus opens as its own page.
