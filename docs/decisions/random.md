# Random

**Scoped and balanced** (decided 2026-10-05). Code: `RandomScope`.

- The library, collection and work pages roll in that page.
- The reader's ⋯ menu shows "Random · *innermost collection*" (or the work, if it has no collection), and a ▾ beside it lists the other enclosing scopes (the work, each collection out to the top, the whole library). For a user's own text it defaults to Your Texts (see [user-texts.md](user-texts.md)).
- Rolls are **balanced**: at each level they pick evenly among a collection's non-empty child collections and works, treating a single-work collection (the Bible, the Book of Mormon) as one branch that is even by verse inside.
- In prose a roll lands on any sentence, chosen evenly like a verse (not snapped to a paragraph's start).
- Every landing stays in history so a chance find can be found again.
- **A shelf is a scope** (2026-10-12): evenly among what's on it, then balanced inside each collection as above. The ▾ lists, under "Your shelves", the shelves holding the page's work or collection, and every shelf from the library. See [shelves.md](shelves.md).

Tried and dropped the same day: an "Even" weighting option (needless complication), and an "Another" chip in the reader to roll again in the same scope (the label didn't say what it referred to; the menu does the job).
