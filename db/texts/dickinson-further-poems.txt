section: 1
label: I fear a man of scanty speech
1. I fear a man of scanty speech,
I fear a silent man,
Haranguer I can overtake
Or babbler entertain—
2. But he who waiteth
While the rest
Expend their inmost pound,
Of this Man I am wary—
I fear that He is Grand.

section: 2
label: Publication is the auction
1. Publication is the auction
Of the mind of man,
Poverty be justifying
For so foul a thing.
2. Possibly,—but we would rather
From our garret go
White unto the White Creator,
Than invest our snow.
3. Thought belongs to Him who gave it—
Then to him who bear
Its corporeal illustration. Sell
The Royal air
In the parcel. Be the merchant
Of the Heavenly Grace,
But reduce no human spirit
To disgrace of price!

section: 3
label: Some work for Immortality
1. Some work for Immortality,
The chiefer part of Time;
He compensates immediately
The former checks on Fame.
2. Slow gold, but everlasting,
The bullion of To-day
Contrasted with the currency
Of Immortality.
3. A beggar here and there
Is gifted to discern
Beyond the broker’s insight—
One’s Money—One’s the Mine.

section: 4
label: The popular Heart is a
1. The popular Heart is a
Cannon first,
Subsequent a drum;
Bells for an auxiliary
And an afterward of rum.
2. Not a to-morrow to know its name,
Nor a past to stare,
Ditches for realms and
A trip to jail
For a souvenir!

section: 5
label: Funny to be a Century
1. Funny to be a Century
And see the people going by,
I should die of the oddity,
But then I’m not so
Staid as he.
2. He keeps his secrets safely, very—
Were he to tell, extremely sorry
This bashful globe of ours
Would be,
So dainty of publicity!

section: 6
label: I cannot dance upon my toes
1. I cannot dance upon my toes,
No man instructed me,
But often times among my mind
A glee possesseth me
That had I ballet knowledge
Would put itself abroad
In pirouette to blanch a troupe,
Or lay a Prima mad!
And though I had no gown of gauze,
No ringlet to my hair,
Nor hopped for audiences like birds,
One claw upon the air,—
Nor tossed my shape in
Eider balls,
Nor rolled on wheels of snow
Till I was out of sight in sound,
The house encored me so—
Nor any knew I know the art
I mention easy here—
Nor any placard boast me,
It ‘s full as opera!

section: 7
label: ’Tis opposites entice
1. ’Tis opposites entice,
Deformed men ponder grace,
Bright fires, the blanketless—
The lost, Day’s face.
2. The blind esteem it be
Enough estate to see;
The captive strangles new
For deeming beggars play.
3. To lack enamour Thee,
Tho’ the Divinity
Be only
Me.

section: 8
label: Color, Caste, Denomination
1. Color, Caste, Denomination—
These are Time’s affair,
Death’s division classifying
Does not know they are.
2. As in sleep—all here forgotten,
Tenets put behind,
Death’s large democratic fingers
Rub away the brand.
3. If Circassian—He is careless—
If He put away
Chrysalis of Blonde or Umber,
Equal butterfly—
4. They emerge from His obscuring,
What Death knows so well,
Our minuter intuitions
Deem incredible.

section: 9
label: I reckon, when I count at all
1. I reckon, when I count at all,
First Poets—then the Sun—
Then Summer—then the
Heaven of God—
And then the list is done.
But looking back—the first so seems
To comprehend the whole—
The others look a needless show,
So I write Poets—All.
This summer lasts a solid year,
They can afford a sun
The East would deem
Extravagant,
And if the final Heaven
Be beautiful as they disclose
To those who trust in them,
It is too difficult a grace
To justify the dream.

section: 10
label: This was a Poet
1. This was a Poet—
It is that
Distills amazing sense
From ordinary
Meanings,
And attars so immense
From the familiar species
That perished by the door,
We wonder it was not
Ourselves
Arrested it before.
2. Of pictures the discloser—
The Poet, it is he,
Entitles us by contrast
To ceaseless poverty.
3. Of portion so unconscious
The robbing could not harm,
Himself, to him, a fortune
Exterior to Time.

section: 11
label: Strong draughts of their refreshing minds
1. Strong draughts of their refreshing minds
To drink, enables mine
Through desert or the
Wilderness,
As bore it sealéd wine—
To go elastic, or as One
The camel’s trait attained,
How powerful the stimulus
Of an hermetic mind!

section: 12
label: We miss a kinsman more
1. We miss a kinsman more
When warranted to see
Than when withheld by oceans
From possibility.
2. A furlong than a league
Inflicts a pricklier pain—
Till we, who smiled at Pyrenees,
Of parishes complain!

section: 13
label: Who giants know, with
1. Who giants know, with
Lesser men
Are incomplete and shy,
For Greatness—that is ill at ease
In minor company.
2. A smaller could not be perturbed,
The summer gnat displays
Unconscious that his single sail
Does not comprise the sky.

section: 14
label: Growth of Man like growth
1. Growth of Man like growth
Of Nature
Gravitates within,
Atmosphere and sun confirm it
But it stirs alone.
2. Each its difficult ideal
Must achieve itself,
Through the solitary prowess
Of a silent life.
3. Effort is the sole condition,
Patience of itself—
Patience of opposing forces,
And distinct belief.
4. Looking on is the department
Of its audience,
But transaction is assisted
By no countenance.

section: 15
label: Doom is the House Without the Door
1. Doom is the House Without the Door—
‘T is entered from the sun,
And then the ladder’s thrown away
Because escape is done.
2. ‘T is varied by the dream
Of what they do outside,
When squirrels play and berries die—
And hundreds bow to God.

section: 16
label: Experience is the angled road
1. Experience is the angled road
Preferred against the mind
By paradox, the mind itself
Presuming it to lead
Quite opposite. How complicate
The discipline of man,
Compelling him to choose himself
His pre-appointed pain.

section: 17
label: I cautious scanned my little life
1. I cautious scanned my little life
I winnowed what would fade
From what would last till heads like mine
Should be a-dreaming laid.
2. I put the latter in a barn,
The former blew away—
I went one winter morning,
And lo! my priceless hay
3. Was not upon the “scaffold”,
Was not upon the “beam”,
And from a thriving farmer
A cynic I became.
4. Whether a thief did it—
Whether it was the wind—
Whether Deity ‘s guiltless
My business is to find.
5. So I begin to ransack—
How is it, Heart, with thee?
Art thou within the little barn
Love provided thee?

section: 18
label: A bird is of all beings
1. A bird is of all beings
The likest to the dawn,
An easy breeze does put afloat
The general Heavens upon.
2. It soars and shifts and whirls
And measures with the clouds
In easy, ever dazzling pace,
No different the birds—
3. Except a wake of music
Accompany their feet,
As should the Dawn emit a tune
For ecstasy of it.

section: 19
label: A prison gets to be a friend
1. A prison gets to be a friend;
Between its ponderous face
And ours a kinsmanship exists,
And in its narrow eyes
We come to look with gratitude
For the appointed beam
It deals us—stated as our food,
And hungered for the same.
2. We learn to know the planks
That answer to our feet,
So miserable a sound at first
Nor even now so sweet
As plashing in the pools
When memory was a boy,
But a demurer circuit,
A geometric joy.
3. The posture of the key
That interrupts the day
To our endeavor,—not so real
The cheek of Liberty
As this companion steel,
Whose features day and night
Are present to us as our own
And as escapeless quite.
4. The narrow round, the stint,
The slow exchange of hope
For something passiver, content
Too steep for looking up,
The liberty we knew
Avoided like a dream,
Too wide for any night but Heaven,
If that indeed redeem.

section: 20
label: Who court obtain
1. Who court obtain
Within himself
Sees every man a king;
So poverty of monarchy
Is an interior thing.
2. No fate depose
Whom Fate ordain—
And who can add a crown
To him who doth continual
Repudiate his own?

section: 21
label: The child’s faith is new
1. The child’s faith is new,
Whole—like his principle—
Wide—like the sunrise
On fresh eyes;
Never had a doubt,
Laughs at a scruple,
Believes all sham
But Paradise!
2. Audits the world—
Deems his dominion
Broadest of sovreignties,
And Caesar mean
By comparison,
Baseless emperor,
Ruler of naught,
Yet swaying all!
3. Grown by and by
To hold mistaken
His pretty estimate
Of prickly things,
He gains the skill
Sorrowful as certain,
Men to propitiate
Instead of kings.

section: 22
label: Forever is composed of Nows
1. Forever is composed of Nows—
‘T is not a different time,
Except for infiniteness
And latitude of home.
2. From this, experienced here,
Remove the dates to these,
Let months dissolve in further months,
And years exhale in years.
3. Without certificate or pause
Or celebrated days,
As infinite our years would be
As Anno Domini’s.

section: 23
label: A science—so the savants say
1. A science—so the savants say,
“Comparative Anatomy”,
By which a single bone
Is made a secret to unfold
Of some rare tenant of the mold
Else perished in the stone.
So to the eye prospective led
This meekest flower of the mead,
Upon a winter’s day,
Stands representative in gold
Of rose and lily, marigold
And countless butterfly!

section: 24
label: To offer brave assistance
1. To offer brave assistance
To lives that stand alone
When one has failed to stop them
Is human,—but divine
To lend an ample sinew
Unto a nameless man
Whose homely benediction
No other cared to earn.

section: 25
label: Drama’s vitalest expression
1. Drama’s vitalest expression
Is the Common Day
That arises, sets, about us:
Other tragedy
Perish in the recitation,
This the more exert
When the audience is scattered,
And the boxes shut.
2. Hamlet to himself were Hamlet
Had not Shakespeare wrote,
Though the Romeo leave no second
Of his Juliet,
It were tenderer enacted
In the human heart—
Only theater recorded
Owner cannot shut.

section: 26
label: A secret told
1. A secret told
Ceases to be a secret then.
A secret kept—
That can appal but one.
Better of it continual be afraid,
Than it
And whom you told it to
Beside.

section: 27
label: I dwell in Possibility
1. I dwell in Possibility
A fairer house than Prose,
More numerous of windows,
Superior of doors.
2. Of chambers, as the cedars—
Impregnable of eye;
And for an everlasting roof
The gables of the sky.
3. Of visitors—the fairest—
For occupation—this—
The spreading wide my narrow hands
To gather Paradise.

section: 28
label: Expectation is contentment
1. Expectation is contentment;
Gain, satiety.
But satiety, conviction
Of necessity.
2. Of an austere trait in pleasure.
Good, without alarm,
Is a too serene possession—
Danger deepens suns.

section: 29
label: She dealt her pretty
1. She dealt her pretty
Words like blades;
As glittering they shone,
And every one unbared
A nerve
Or wantoned with a bone.
2. She never deemed she hurt;
That is not steel’s
Affair.
A vulgar grimace in
The flesh
How ill the creatures bear!
3. To ache is human, not
Polite;
The film upon the eye
Mortality’s old custom—
Just locking up to die.

section: 30
label: Revolution is the pod
1. Revolution is the pod
Systems rattle from;
When the winds of
Will are stirred,
Excellent is bloom.
2. But except its russet
Base
Every summer be
The entomber of itself;
So of Liberty.
3. Left inactive on the
Stalk,
All its purple fled,
Revolution shakes it
For
Test if it be dead.

section: 31
label: I’ve known a Heaven like a tent
1. I’ve known a Heaven like a tent
To wrap its shining yards,
Pluck up its stakes and disappear
Without the sound of boards
Or rip of nail, or carpenter,
But just the miles of stare
That signalize a show’s retreat
In North America.
No trace, no figment of the thing
That dazzled yesterday,
No ring, no marvel;
Men and feats
Dissolved as utterly
As birds’ far navigation
Discloses just a hue;
A plash of oars—a gayety,
Then swallowed up to view.

section: 32
label: We see comparatively.
1. We see comparatively.
The thing so towering high
We could not grasp its segment
Unaided yesterday.
2. This morning’s finer verdict
Makes scarcely worth the toil—
A furrow our Cordillera,
Our Apennines a knoll.

section: 33
label: A still volcano—Life
1. A still volcano—Life—
That flickered in the night
When it was dark enough to show
Without endangering sight.
2. A quiet, earthquake style,
Too smoldering to suspect
By natures this side Naples.
The North cannot detect
3. The solemn, torrid symbol,
The lips that never lie,
Whose hissing corals part and shut
And cities slip away.
4. Therefore we do Life’s labor
Tho’ Life’s reward be done—
With scrupulous exactness
To hold our senses on.

section: 34
label: To make routine
1. To make routine
A stimulus,
Remember it can cease—
Capacity to terminate
Is a specific
Grace.

section: 35
label: It’s easy to invent a life
1. It’s easy to invent a life,
God does it every day—
Creation but a gambol
Of His authority.
2. It’s easy to efface it,
The thrifty Deity
Could scarce afford eternity
To spontaneity.
3. The Perished Patterns murmur,
But His perturbless plan
Proceed—inserting here
A Sun—
There—leaving out a Man.

section: 36
label: The sweetest heresy received
1. The sweetest heresy received
That man and woman know,
Each other’s convert—
Though the faith accommodate but two.
2. The churches are so frequent,
The ritual so small,
The Grace so unavoidable,
To fail—is infidel.

section: 37
label: I never felt at home below
1. I never felt at home below,
And in the handsome skies
I shall not feel at home
I know,
I don’t like Paradise.
2. Because it’s Sunday all the time
And recess never comes,
And Eden’ll be so lonesome
Bright Wednesday afternoons.
3. If God could make a visit,
Or ever took a nap—
So not to see us—but they say
Himself a telescope
4. Perennial beholds us,—
Myself would run away
From Him and Holy Ghost and All—
But—there’s the Judgment Day!

section: 38
label: Of course I prayed
1. Of course I prayed—
And did God care?
He cared as much as
On the air
A bird had stamped her foot
And cried “Give me!”
2. My reason, life
I had not had, but for
Yourself,
’Twere better charity
To leave me in the atom’s
Tomb,
Merry and nought, and gay
And numb,
Than this smart misery.

section: 39
label: I prayed at first—a little girl
1. I prayed at first—a little girl—
Because they told me to,
But stopped when qualified to guess
How prayer would sound to me
2. If I supposed God looked around
Each time my childish eye
Fixed full and steady on His own
In solemn honesty—
3. And told Him what I’d like to-day,
And parts of His far plan
That baffled me—the underside
Of His divinity.
4. And often since in danger
I count the force ‘t would be
To have a God so strong as that,
To hold my life for me,
5. Till I could catch the balance
That slips so easily;
It takes me all the while to poise,
And then it doesn’t stay.

section: 40
label: It always felt to me a wrong
1. It always felt to me a wrong
To that old Moses done,
To let him see the Canaan
Without the entering.
2. And though in soberer moments
No Moses there can be,
I’m satisfied the romance
In point of injury
3. Surpasses sharper stated
Of Stephen or of Paul;
For these were only put to death,
While God’s adroiter will
4. On Moses seemed to fasten
In tantalizing play—
As Boy should deal
With lesser Boy
To show supremacy.
5. The fault was doubtless
Israel’s;
Myself had banned the Tribes,
And ushered grand old Moses
In pentateuchal robes
6. Upon the broad possession
But titled him to see.
Old Man on Nebo! Late as this
One Justice bleeds for thee!

section: 41
label: My period had come for prayer
1. My period had come for prayer,
No other art would do,
My tactics missed a rudiment;
Creator, was it you?
2. God grows above, so those who pray
Horizons must ascend,
And so I stood upon the North
To reach this curious Friend.
3. His house was not; no sign had He
By chimney nor by door,—
Could I infer His residence?
Wide prairies of the air
4. Unbroken by a settler,
Were all that I could see;
Infinitude, hadst Thou no face
That I might look at Thee?
5. The silence condescended,
The Heavens paused for me,
But awed beyond my errand
I worshiped—did not pray!

section: 42
label: We pray to Heaven
1. We pray to Heaven,
We prate of Heaven—
Relate when neighbors die,
At what o’clock to Heaven
They fled.
Who saw them wherefore fly?
2. Is Heaven a place, and Sky a face?
Location’s narrow way
Is for ourselves;
Unto the Dead
There’s no geography.

section: 43
label: “Unto Me?”
1. “Unto Me?”
“I do not know you—
Where may be your house?”
2. “I am Jesus—late of
Judea,
Now of Paradise.”
3. “Wagons have you, to
Convey me?
This is far from thence”—
4. “Arms of mine sufficient
Phaeton,
Trust Omnipotence.”
5. “I am spotted.”
“I am Pardon.”
“I am small.”
“The least
Is esteemed in Heaven
The chiefest.
Occupy my house.”

section: 44
label: Too much of proof affronts
1. Too much of proof affronts
Belief.
The Turtle will not try
Unless you leave him;
Then return—
And he has hauled away.

section: 45
label: The Sun went down
1. The Sun went down—
No man looked on;
The Earth and I alone
Were present at the majesty,
He triumphed and went on.
2. The Sun went up—
No man looked on,
The Earth and I and One—
A nameless bird, a stranger,
Were witness for the Crown.

section: 46
label: The tint I cannot take is best
1. The tint I cannot take is best,
The color too remote
That I could show it in bazaar
A guinea at a sight—
2. The fine impalpable array
That swaggers on the eye
Like Cleopatra’s company
Repeated in the sky—
3. The moments of dominion
That happen on the Soul
And leave it with a discontent
Too exquisite to tell—
4. The eager look on landscapes
As if they just repressed
Some secret that was pushing,
Like chariots, in the breast—
5. The pleading of the Summer,
That other prank of snow
That covers mystery with tulle
For fear the squirrels know—
6. Their graspless manners mock us,
Until the cheated eye
Shuts arrogantly in the grave,
Another way to see.

section: 47
label: Heaven has different signs to me
1. Heaven has different signs to me;
Sometimes I think that noon
Is but a symbol of the place,
And when again at dawn
2. A mighty look runs round the world
And settles in the hills,
An awe if it should be like that
Upon the ignorance steals.
3. The orchard when the sun is on;
The triumph of the birds
When they together victory make;
Some carnivals of clouds—
4. The rapture of concluded day
Returning to the West,—
All these remind us of the place
That men call “Paradise.”
5. Itself a fairer we suppose,
But how ourself shall be
Adorned for a superior grace,
Not yet our eyes can see.

section: 48
label: The rainbow never tells me
1. The rainbow never tells me
That gust and storm are by;
Yet is she more convincing
Than philosophy.
2. My flowers turn from forums,
Yet eloquent declare
What Cato couldn’t prove to me
Except the birds were here!

section: 49
label: Beauty is not caused
1. Beauty is not caused,
It is.
Chase it and it ceases.
Chase it not and it abides.
Overtake the creases
In the meadow when
The Wind
Runs his fingers thro’ it?
Deity will see to it
That you never do it.

section: 50
label: My faith is larger than the hills
1. My faith is larger than the hills,
So when the hills decay,
My faith must take the purple wheel
To show the Sun the way.
2. ‘T is first he steps upon the vane
And then upon the hill;
And then abroad the world he goes
To do his golden will.
3. And if his yellow feet should miss,
The birds would not arise,
The flowers would slumber on their stems,—
No bells have Paradise.
4. How dare I therefore stint a faith
On which so vast depends,
Lest Firmament should fail for me—
The rivet in the bands.

section: 51
label: Within my garden rides a bird
1. Within my garden rides a bird
Upon a single wheel,
Whose spokes a dizzy music make
As ‘t were a traveling mill.
2. He never stops, but slackens
Above the ripest rose,
Partakes without alighting,
And praises as he goes;
3. Till every spice is tasted,
And then his fairy gig
Reels in remoter atmospheres,
And I rejoin my dog.
4. And he and I perplex us
If positive ‘t were we—
Or bore the garden in the brain
This curiosity?
5. But he, the best logician,
Refers my duller eye
To just vibrating blossoms—
An exquisite reply!

section: 52
label: The Robin’s my criterion of tune
1. The Robin’s my criterion of tune
Because I grow where robins do—
But were I Cuckoo born
I’d swear by him—
The ode familiar rules the morn.
The Buttercup’s my whim
For bloom—
Because we’re orchard-sprung—
But were I Britain-born
I’d daisies spurn—
None but the Nut October fits
Because through dropping it
The seasons flit, I’m taught.
Without the snow’s tableau
Winter were lie to me—
Because I see New Englandly.
The Queen discerns like me
Provincially.

section: 53
label: We—Bee and I—live
1. We—Bee and I—live
In the quaffing.
‘T isn’t all hock with us,
Life has its ale—
But it’s many a lay of
The dim Burgundy
We chant for cheer when
The wines fail.
Do we “get drunk?”
Ask the jolly clovers!
Do we “beat our wife?”
I never wed.
Bee pledges his in minutest
Flagons,
Dainty as the tress on her
Deft head.
While runs the Rhine
He and I revel—
First at the vat and
Latest at the vine;
Noon—our last cup.
“Found dead of nectar”
By a humming Coroner
In a by-thyme.

section: 54
label: A fuzzy fellow without feet
1. A fuzzy fellow without feet
Yet doth exceeding run!
Of velvet is his countenance
And his complexion dun.
2. Sometimes he dwelleth in the grass,
Sometimes upon a bough
From which he doth descend in plush
Upon the passer-by.
3. All this in summer—
But when winds alarm the forest folk,
He taketh damask residence
And struts in sewing silk.
4. Then, finer than a lady,
Emerges in the spring,
A feather on each shoulder—
You’d scarce accredit him.
5. By men yelept a caterpillar—
By me—but who am I
To tell the pretty secret
Of the Butterfly!

section: 55
label: If Nature smiles—the Mother must
1. If Nature smiles—the Mother must,
I’m sure, at many a whim
Of her eccentric family—
Is she so much to blame?

section: 56
label: To intercept his yellow plan
1. To intercept his yellow plan
The sun does not allow
Caprices of the atmosphere;
And even when the snow
2. Heaves balls of specks like vicious boy
Directly in his eye,
Does not so much as turn his head—
Busy with majesty!
3. ’Tis his to stimulate the earth,
And magnetize the sea,
And bind astronomy in place—
Yet any passer-by
4. Would deem Ourselves the busier,
As the minutest bee
That rides supports a thunder,
A bomb to justify!

section: 57
label: By my window have I for scenery
1. By my window have I for scenery
Just a sea with a stem—
If the bird and the farmer deem it a “Pine”,
The opinion will serve for them.
2. It has no “Port”, nor a “Line”, but the jays
That split their route to the sky,
Or a squirrel whose giddy peninsular
May be easier gained this way.
3. For inlands the Earth is the underside
And the upper side is the Sun,
And its commerce—if commerce it have—
Of spice, I infer from the odors borne.
4. Of its voice to affirm, when the wind is within
Can the dumb divulge the Divine?
The definition of melody is
That definition is none.
5. It suggests to our faith, they suggest to our sight,—
When the latter is put away,
I shall meet with conviction I somewhere met
That Immortality.
6. Was the Pine at my
Window a “Fellow”
Of the Royal Infinity?
Apprehensions are God’s
Introductions
Extended inscrutably.

section: 58
label: Out of sight? What of that?
1. Out of sight? What of that?
See the bird reach it!
Curve on curve, sweep on sweep,
Round the steep air.
Danger! What is that to her?
Better ’tis to fail there
Than debate here.
2. Blue is blue the world through,
Amber, amber; dew, dew.
Seek friend, and see—
Heaven is sky of earth
That’s all—
Bashful Heaven, thy lovers small
Hide too, from thee.

section: 59
label: When they come back
1. When they come back,
If Blossoms do—
I always feel a doubt
If Blossoms can be
Born again
When once the art
Is out.
2. When they begin,
If Robins may—
I always had a fear
I did not tell, it
Was their last
Experiment
Last year.
3. When it is May,
If May return—
Had nobody a pang

11. To disappear enhances;
The man who runs away
Is tinctured for an instant
With Immortality.
12. But yesterday a vagrant,
Today in memory lain
With superstitious merit
We tamper with again.
13. But never far as Honour
Removes the paltry One,
And impotent to cherish
We hasten to adorn.
14. Of Death the sharpest function,
That, just as we discern,
The Excellence defies us;
Securest gathered then
15. The fruit perverse to plucking,
But leaning to the sight
With the ecstatic limit
Of unobtained Delight.

section: 177
label: God is a distant, stately Lover
1. God is a distant, stately Lover,
Woos, so He tells us, by His Son.
Surely a vicarious courtship!
Miles’ and Priscilla’s such a one.
But lest the soul, like fair Priscilla,
Choose the envoy and spurn the Groom,
Vouches, with hyperbolic archness,
Miles and John Alden
Are synonym.

section: 178
label: A feather from the whippoorwill
1. A feather from the whippoorwill
That everlasting sings,
Whose galleries are sunrise,
Whose stanzas are the spring,
Whose emerald nest the ages spin
With mellow murmuring thread,
Whose beryl egg what schoolboys hunt
In “recess” overhead!

section: 179
label: There are two ripenings
1. There are two ripenings—
One of sight, whose forces
Spheric wind,
Until the velvet product
Drops, spicy, to the ground.
2. A homelier maturing,
The process in the burr—
That teeth of frosts
Alone disclose
On far October air.

section: 180
label: The zeros taught us phosphorus
1. The zeros taught us phosphorus,
We learned to like the fire
By handling glaciers when a boy,
And tinder guessed by power
Of opposite to equal ought,
Eclipses sums imply,
Paralysis our primer numb
Unto vitality.

section: 181
label: Just once! Oh, least request!
1. Just once! Oh, least request!
Could Adamant refuse
So small a grace
So scanty put—
Such agonizing terms?
Would not a God of Flint
Be conscious of a sigh
As down His Heaven
Dropt, remote,
“Just once! Sweet Deity?”
